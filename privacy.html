MapHarvest Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

MapHarvest is a Chrome extension that builds a list of businesses from your own Google Maps searches. It has no server, no account and no analytics.

What MapHarvest stores

The businesses it collects (names, categories, ratings, phones, websites, addresses, coordinates), contact details it finds on those businesses' websites, and your license key. All of it stays in your browser's extension storage on your computer until you press "Clear" or remove the extension.

What leaves your computer

  • Nothing about you or your leads is sent to the developer.
  • When you activate Pro, your license key and an activation id are sent to Polar (polar.sh), our payment provider, to check your subscription; it is re-checked about once a day.
  • When you use "Find emails & socials" (Pro), MapHarvest opens the public websites of the businesses in your list, without cookies or any data about you, and reads publicly listed emails and social links.

What MapHarvest does not do

It does not read pages other than Google Maps and, on request, the listed businesses' websites. It does not sell or share data, show ads, or track browsing.

Your responsibility

Business contact details are personal data in some countries. Use your lists in line with anti-spam and data-protection laws that apply to you.
